WHY THE MENU MATTERS

Contradictions
close the circuit.

01. A crib creates links

Each aligned plaintext and ciphertext letter becomes a link through an Enigma scrambler at that message position. Repeated letters and loops connect these links into a menu.

02. Reciprocal cables propagate

If the plugboard connects A to B, it must also connect B to A. A proposed connection propagates through the menu. If a letter is forced to have two partners, that branch is rejected.

03. A stop needs checking

This educational solver searches real Enigma windows and propagates reciprocal plugboard constraints. It uses bounded backtracking rather than reconstructing every electrical relay of a historical Bombe. It returns one compatible wiring per surviving window setting; unconstrained letters are left unplugged. Other wirings can exist.

Hard branches exceeding 2,000 solver nodes are reported as unresolved, not rejected. Results stop at 100 candidates. A completed scan with unresolved settings is not an exhaustive proof of failure.
